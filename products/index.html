How CheckAm works

Three steps. A few seconds.

Save the number

Save +234 703 831 7433 as CheckAm, or use the checker on our website.

Forward what worries you

A message, link, account number and bank, phone number or a seller's page.

Get a verdict with reasons

Likely scam, Caution, Unverified or Looks fine, and exactly why, plus what to do next.

Why trust CheckAm

Evidence, not guesses.

We are careful because a wrong flag can hurt an honest person. These rules apply to every product we build.

Every verdict has reasons

We say "reported by 3 people", never "this person is a fraudster". "No warning signs" is never a guarantee.

One report never flags anyone

Two or more different people must report before others are warned, and reports are reviewed.

A fair appeal route

Flagged wrongly? Email hello@openfraudlabs.com and we review it. Reporters are never named.

Your data stays minimal

Numbers are stored as scrambled codes. We never ask for a PIN, OTP, password or BVN. Built to follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

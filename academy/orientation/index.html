Inside every lesson

The same four steps, about an hour each lesson

01

Watch

A 10-minute video with worked Python examples

02

Read

Study notes with the code, key terms and common mistakes

03

Practise

Three coding exercises in your browser or in Google Colab, nothing to install

04

Quiz

Score 70% to pass and unlock the next lesson

Missed the mark? Review the notes and try again. Worked solutions appear once you pass.

Each step unlocks the next, so finishing a lesson means you really did the work. The practice exercises must be solved before the quiz opens. Budget about 45 to 60 minutes per lesson including practice.

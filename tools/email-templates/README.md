# Academy auth email templates

Paste each file into Supabase → Authentication → Emails → Templates (Source/HTML view), with the subject below.

- **confirm-signup.html** — subject: `Confirm your email for Open Fraud Labs Academy`
- **reset-password.html** — subject: `Reset your Open Fraud Labs Academy password`
- **change-email.html** — subject: `Confirm your new email address`
- **magic-link.html** — subject: `Your Open Fraud Labs Academy sign-in link`

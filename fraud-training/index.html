What we offer

Four ways we can help your organisation

For all staff

Scam-awareness session

A practical session on the scams hitting Nigerians now: fake alerts, impersonation of banks and bosses, phishing links, OTP and PIN tricks, job and loan-fee scams.

  • Real examples, with the names removed
  • A short quiz to check understanding
  • A one-page "check before you pay" guide for staff
For lenders and MFBs

Fraud process review

We walk through your onboarding, disbursement, collections and staff controls, and show where fraud, including insider abuse, can get through.

  • Interviews with your team and a look at your process
  • A written report with risks ranked
  • Practical fixes you can start this month
For data and risk teams

Fraud analytics workshop

Hands-on training in finding fraud in your own data with SQL and Python, and building risk models your team can explain.

  • Fraud rules and red-flag queries
  • Explainable models with SHAP
  • Exercises on sample data
For finance and front-line staff

Cyber-safety session

How attackers trick staff into handing over access or moving money, and the simple checks that stop them.

  • Phishing emails and fake login pages
  • Fake supplier invoices and "new bank details" requests
  • CEO and boss impersonation on email and WhatsApp
  • SIM swap and OTP theft

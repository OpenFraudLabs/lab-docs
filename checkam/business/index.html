Where it fits

One check at the moment money or trust changes hands

Fintechs and wallets

Check the beneficiary account before a customer sends money, and warn them in the app if it has been reported.

Lenders and BNPL

Screen phone numbers, accounts and links at onboarding and disbursement, before you lend into a fraud ring.

Marketplaces and e-commerce

Check sellers, vendor accounts and payment links before payouts, and spot fake store pages that copy your brand.

Logistics, PAYG and solar

Check agents, payment numbers and "customer care" pages that fraudsters use to divert customer payments.

What you get

A risk level, and the reason behind it

Every result says why. Your operations team never has to act on a score nobody can explain, and your customers get a fair answer.

Shared scam reports

Reports from the public on WhatsApp and the website, and from partners. Each report is reviewed, and one report alone never flags anyone.

Link and website checks

How new a website is, and whether it copies the address of a real bank or brand.

Known scam scripts

Messages matched against scam patterns we track, including fake alerts, OTP and PIN requests, job and task scams, and loan-fee scams.

Likely scamSeveral different people reported it, and the reports were reviewed.
CautionReports or warning signs worth checking before you pay.
UnverifiedNot enough information either way.
Looks fineNo warning signs found. Not a guarantee, and we say so.

Owners who think they were flagged wrongly can appeal, and we never reveal who made a report.

How the pilot works

Start small, measure it, then connect

Tell us where fraud hurts

A short call about your payouts, onboarding or customer complaints, and what a useful result looks like for your team.

Test it on real cases

Send us a sample of accounts, phone numbers or links, without customer names. We run them through CheckAm and return each result with its reasons.

Connect and share back

Pilot partners get API access as it opens, can send confirmed fraud cases back to strengthen everyone's checks, and help shape pricing.

We share risk levels, report counts and reasons, never the identity of reporters. CheckAm is built to follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. Read the privacy policy.

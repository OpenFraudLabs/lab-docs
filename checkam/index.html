How we decide

Every verdict comes with its reasons

Evidence, not guesses

Verdicts come from reviewed reports, link checks and known scam scripts. We say "reported by 3 people", never "this person is a fraudster".

Honest about limits

No warning signs is not a guarantee. We can't see bank records, so a screenshot check can never confirm that a payment happened.

Your data stays minimal

We store your number only as a scrambled code, never publish names of reported people, and are built to follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act.

Think your account or link was flagged wrongly? Email hello@openfraudlabs.com with the details and we'll review it.

CheckAm Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This policy explains what CheckAm, a scam-checking service from Open Fraud Labs, collects when you use it on WhatsApp or on openfraudlabs.com/checkam, and how we protect it. For our website and Academy, see the Open Fraud Labs Privacy Policy.

Who we are

CheckAm is operated by Open Fraud Labs, based in Lagos, Nigeria. Contact: hello@openfraudlabs.com.

This policy covers the CheckAm WhatsApp service, the CheckAm page at openfraudlabs.com/checkam, and the CheckAm business API. It applies to anyone who sends a message to CheckAm, submits a scam report, or contacts us about a flagged account, phone number or link.

What we collect

We collect only what we need to check messages and warn people about scams.

  • Your WhatsApp number: when you message CheckAm. Stored only as a scrambled code (a keyed hash). We do not store the number itself in our database.
  • Accounts, phone numbers and links: when you send them to be checked or reported, so we can match future checks and count reports.
  • Check results: each time you ask for a check, we keep the verdict, the reasons shown to you, and the time.
  • Report details: when you send REPORT or use the web form, we keep the account, number or link you report and your description of what happened.
  • Feedback: if you tell us whether a verdict helped, we keep your yes/no answer and any comment.
  • Appeal details: if you dispute a flag, we keep the contact details and explanation you give us.
  • Website visits: when you open the CheckAm page, we count the visit, where it came from (for example LinkedIn or Facebook) and whether you tapped a WhatsApp button, with a random code stored in your browser so repeat visits are counted once. We do not use cookies or record your IP address on this page.

What we do not collect: your name, contacts, location, bank login details, PINs, OTPs or BVN. We do not keep the full text of messages you send for checking. Never send us your PIN, OTP, password or BVN.

How we use it

  • to check what you send and reply with a verdict and its reasons
  • to count independent, reviewed reports so we can warn others about the same account, number or link
  • to review appeals and correct mistakes
  • to prevent abuse, such as fake reports or excessive messages
  • to improve how accurately CheckAm spots scams
  • to answer businesses that ask our API whether an account, number or link has been reported

Our legal basis under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 is your consent when you choose to message us, and our legitimate interest in protecting the public from fraud.

What we share and with whom

  • Service providers: Meta, which runs WhatsApp and delivers messages between you and CheckAm, and Supabase, which hosts our database and servers.
  • Business API users: risk levels, report counts and reasons about an account, number or link. We never share who reported it or the reporter's WhatsApp number.
  • Public pages: scam patterns only, such as common scam messages. We never publish the names or personal details of people who are reported.
  • Authorities: only when the law requires it.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

  • Check results: kept for 12 months, then deleted.
  • Reports: kept while they help warn people. Only reports from the last 90 days count toward a warning.
  • Appeals: kept for 12 months after a decision.
  • Your scrambled WhatsApp code: kept until you ask us to delete it, or after 12 months without use.

Security and where data is stored

Our database and servers are hosted by Supabase in London, United Kingdom. Data is encrypted in transit, access is limited to authorised Open Fraud Labs staff, and every report is reviewed before it can affect a warning. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you can ask us to tell you what information we hold about you, correct information that is wrong, delete your information, or stop using it and withdraw your consent.

Deleting your data

Email hello@openfraudlabs.com with the subject “Delete my CheckAm data” and the WhatsApp number you used. We will delete your scrambled number code, your check history and your feedback within 30 days, and confirm by email. Reports you submitted may be kept without any link to you, so they can still protect others.

If you think we have handled your information wrongly, you can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Children

CheckAm is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Important changes will also be announced on openfraudlabs.com/checkam.
